SUGAR & SPITE06/06

Play solo against the Rival Baker

Setup

Play the normal two-player game with the Rival as Player 2. Deal 3 cakes: you pick one, the Rival takes the left-most of the other two, and the third starts the Counter. You go first. Shuffle the six Rival cards and put them face down.

The Rival's turn

  1. Turn its cakes, as you would your own.
  2. Flip the top Rival card. When they run out, shuffle all six again.
  3. Do what the card says.
  4. Refill the Counter.
Winning. Most points wins and a tie goes to the Rival. Casual players win about 38 games in 100, strong players about 69, in simulation.

The usual list: do the first that works

  1. Pull a cake that can't get better: its next number is the same or lower, or it is Dark. Biggest number first.
  2. Slam one of your Batter or Risen cakes if skipping a stage would cost it 3 or more (not a Water bath). Use the Counter card with the smallest biggest-number.
  3. Bake if it has an empty rack: the Counter card with the biggest number on it.
  4. Bin the left-most Counter card.

If the Counter is empty it pulls its best cake, or skips. Ties go to the left-most card.

Effects

It scores Tip jar and Glaze, plays Draught on your cake that would lose most, and plays Chef's pick and Reheat (bakes the card if it has a free rack, otherwise bins it). It ignores every other effect.
