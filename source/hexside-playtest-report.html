1 Pace +1, all five positions Adopted

2 Emergency Goalkeeper sin-bin bug Fixed

Both playtest drivers deputized an Emergency Goalkeeper on any routine sin-bin trip, not just a genuine send-off — the real Goalkeeper returning from the sin-bin then collided with its own still-present deputy and crashed the match outright. Root cause: the turn-start safety net checked "is the Goalkeeper simply absent from the pitch," which is equally true during a temporary sin-bin as a permanent dismissal.

The same bug class had already been fixed once on the digital board earlier in the project — this was an independent recurrence in the Python reference engine, found incidentally while running an unrelated Pace trial. Fixed in both driver.py and driver_base.py by checking send-off status specifically, not mere absence.

  • Both drivers smoke-tested clean at N=50 after the fix
  • Full N=1000 batch immediately after, zero crashes — sin-bin trips still occurring at the same rate, no longer colliding

3 Bounce Pass — new mechanic In the rulebook

A Pass may now be played off a side or back wall instead of straight at a teammate, reflecting once before landing. Worked out from first principles this session: the pitch's straight lines run on four axes (vertical, horizontal, and two diagonals), and reflecting any of them off either wall type always lands on another one of the same four — a closed system. Only the two diagonals actually redirect; a vertical or horizontal line either bounces straight back off its own matching wall or can't reach the other type at all.

One exception drives the whole mechanic's appeal: the two goal-mouth hexes at each end have no wall on any axis. A line reaching one of them keeps going into the net instead of bouncing, resolved exactly like a Shot — Save-contested, never automatic. Pass Range is spent across both legs of the bend combined, the same way a straight Pass counts every hex it crosses.

Getting the AI to actually use it

Fairly ranked against a normal Pass, a bounce almost never won — it's locked to one of eight fixed lines from wherever the carrier stands, while a straight Pass can aim at any teammate on four axes. A deliberate ranking discount fixed the teammate case, but the empty-hex case needed something sharper: shrinking the discount didn't create a middle ground, it flipped outcomes from 100% one way to 100% the other at any fixed value. The two options are almost always tied in real positions, so no fixed number could split them — only coin-flipping whether the discount applies at all produced a genuine mix.

4 Slide Tackle → Strong Tackle Renamed

Real five-a-side football doesn't allow sliding tackles, so a card named after one was describing an illegal challenge. Renamed throughout the project — engine, both drivers, the printed cards, and the rulebook — with the mechanics completely untouched: +1 Attack die, free to play, double Discipline draw on a loss.

Not a pure find-and-replace: a handful of sentences used "slide" as ordinary prose for the physical motion itself, not just the card's proper name, and needed rewriting rather than substitution. The digital board Artifact was checked directly and doesn't reference the card at all — it implements the Base Game's Command Deck only, with no Tactic Deck to have ever included it.
