Rules note

Faithful to the printed Base Game rulebook: real Command Card hand & deck, dice-based Pass/Dribble/Shot/Tackle resolution (including Bounce Pass, §8), Save-retention & rebounds, Discipline deck & sin-bin, and optional Character Skins with all 12 Signatures live (Showman needs a real Tactic Deck, which Base Game never deals, so it structurally can't trigger here — not a gap) — and a real hand-played kickoff-placement draft at kickoff, every goal, and half-time: you name your own taker and place your own units, alternating with the CPU, reacting to what's already on the board. One simplification for this digital build: the CPU plays sound but not optimal tactics.
