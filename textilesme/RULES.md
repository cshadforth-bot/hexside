# Textilesme (prototype v0.1)
Solo, 18 cards, ~15 minutes. Turn old clothes into six commissions with as little waste as you can.

**Cards:** 12 Salvage (a 2x2 grid of fabric patches; some have a moth hole) + 6 Commissions (a garment with 4 slots).

**Setup:** Shuffle the Salvage deck; deal 4 face up as the Rack. Shuffle the Commissions; you will do all six in turn.

**A commission (up to 4 Cuts):** Each Cut, pick a Rack card and cut either one patch (fills a 1-wide slot)
or two edge-adjacent patches (fills a 2-wide slot). Cut patches are used up (cross them off / cover them).
Holes cannot be used. Diagonal patches are not adjacent.

**Scoring a commission:** 1 per filled slot, +1 if the patch(es) match the slot's requirement (colour or texture; a slot with no
requirement always gets it), +4 style bonus if all four slots are filled and the Style rule is met. Max 12.

**Between commissions:** discard Rack cards with no usable patches left, then add 2 Salvage cards (after commissions 1-4).
The 12 cards are therefore all seen by commission 5.

**Result (max 72):** under 35 Tip pile - 35-47 Charity shop - 48-59 Market stall - 60+ Runway.
