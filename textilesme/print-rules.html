18-card sewing game · sister to Pour Decisions, Carly Cakes and First Crack

Textilesme

You run a one-bench tailoring studio and nothing you sew is new. Build garments layer by layer out of other people's old clothes, and scrap what you can't use before your rival can. Day 2 is played with the clothes you finished on day 1.

Draft 0.31-2 tailors20 minutes18 cardsAges 10+

Every card is two things

A garment order (a name, a recipe of 2-4 fabrics and 2, 4 or 6 tips) and a fabric source. The top strip is the garment's own first fabric. The bottom strip is a salvage patch, printed upside down. Recipes read bottom to top and are numbered on the garment.

Setup

Shuffle all 18 cards. Each player takes one as their first open garment, its top strip already its first layer. Deal 4 cards face up: the Counter (3 in solo). The rest is the deck. Player 1 goes first.

Your turn: take one Counter card and do one thing with it

DesignStart a new garment with it, its top strip is layer 1. You have two workbenches: at most 2 open garments.
StitchSlide it under one of your open garments so one strip peeks out as the next layer. That strip must be the fabric the recipe needs next. You may show either strip.
UnpickScrap it into the face-up scrap bin. Usually to stop your rival finishing a garment.

Then refill the Counter from the deck. When a garment's layers match its recipe it is finished: bank its tips and resolve its effect. Its stitched cards go to the scrap bin. The garment card itself stays in front of you as your score.

Effects (when finished)

Second pair of hands
Take another Counter card and use it.
Moth
Unpick the top stitched layer off one of your rival's open garments. A ticket's own first layer is safe. The card goes to the scrap bin.
Keep the offcuts
Put one stitched card from this garment in your Stash (+1 tip each) instead of the scrap bin.
Rummage
Draw the top card of the deck and use it as if it were on the Counter.
Jumble sale
Scrap the whole Counter, then deal a new one.

Two days, and nothing is wasted

When the deck is empty, keep playing without refills until the Counter is empty. That ends a day.

Washing up. Every open garment is unpicked (-1 tip each) and goes to the scrap bin. Finished garments are resold: they go into the scrap bin too, and your tips stay banked. Shuffle the scrap bin into a new deck and deal a new Counter. Day 2 is played with the clothes you made on day 1. Player 2 starts day 2. Stashed cards leave the game.

Scoring

Tips on finished garments, plus 1 for each card in your Stash, minus 1 for each garment still open at the end of day 2. Most points wins. A tie is a shared win.

Solo

Use a Counter of 3. After each of your turns the oldest Counter card goes cold and leaves the game. Ignore Moth. Play both days and score as above.
