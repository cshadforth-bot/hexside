18-card sewing game · sister to Pour Decisions, Carly Cakes and First Crack

Textilesme

You run a one-bench tailoring studio and nothing you sew is new. Piece a quilt together from other people's old clothes, edge to edge, and match your swatches to score buttons. Day 2 is played with the same clothes, remade.

Draft 0.51-2 tailors20 minutes18 cardsAges 10+

Every card is a patch

Each card is a garment cut into four patches: a swatch on its top, bottom, left and right edges. The garment in the middle is drawn from the same four fabrics, so you can read the edges off the picture. There are 72 swatches, 12 of each fabric.

Setup

Shuffle all 18 cards. Turn the top card face up in the middle of the table: the Foundation. The fabric on its top edge is today's Trend. Deal 4 cards face up: the Rail (3 in solo). The rest is the deck. Player 1 goes first.

Your turn

Take one Rail card and sew it onto the quilt. Lay it next to the cards already there, edge to edge, in a square grid. Cards stay upright, but you may turn one 180 degrees. It must touch at least one card. Then refill the Rail.

Seam: 2 buttonsEach card you touch whose touching edge shows the same fabric scores 2 buttons.
Trend: +1A matching seam in the Trend fabric scores 1 more button.
Perfect fit: +2If your card touches 2 or more cards and every touching edge matches, take 2 more buttons.

Edges that don't match score nothing and cost nothing. The Foundation scores nothing. Every card is sewn: there is no pass and no discard. Taking a card is also how you keep it from your rival.

Example

The Trend is Silk. You sew a card so its left edge (Wool) meets the right edge (Wool) of a card already there: 2 buttons. Its top edge (Silk) meets the bottom edge (Silk) of another card: 2 buttons, plus 1 for the Trend. Both edges match, so it is a perfect fit: 2 more. That turn scores 7 buttons.

Two days, and nothing is wasted

A day ends when the deck and the Rail are empty. Washing up: unpick the whole quilt and shuffle all 18 cards together. Your buttons stay banked. Day 2 is played with the same clothes, remade: a new Foundation, a new Trend, a new Rail. Player 2 starts day 2.

Score. Buttons from both days. Most buttons wins. A tie is a shared win.

Solo

Use a Rail of 3. After each of your turns the oldest Rail card goes cold and leaves the day, so you sew 9 cards a day. Play both days and score as above. A typical game scores around 50.
