18-card sewing game · sister to Pour Decisions, Carly Cakes and First Crack

Textilesme

You run a one-bench tailoring studio and nothing you sew is new. Piece a quilt together from other people's old clothes, edge to edge, and match your swatches to score buttons. Remake what you can't use, and let each collection's effect do its trick. Day 2 is played with the same clothes, remade.

Every card is a patch, and a garment

Each card is a garment cut into four patches: a swatch on its top, bottom, left and right edges. The garment in the middle is drawn from the same four fabrics, so you can read the edges off the picture. There are 72 swatches, 12 of each fabric. Every garment also belongs to a collection, named under its title. All three garments in a collection share one effect, printed on the card and listed on page 2.

Setup

Shuffle all 18 cards. Turn the top card face up in the middle of the table: the Foundation. The fabric on its top edge is today's Trend. Deal 4 cards face up: the Rail (3 in solo). The rest is the deck. Player 1 goes first.

Your turn: take one Rail card and do one thing with it

Sew it onto the quilt. Lay it next to the cards already there, edge to edge, in a square grid. Cards stay upright, but you may turn one 180 degrees. It must touch at least one card.
Upcycle it. Sew it over a card on the quilt from the same collection. The covered card is unpicked and out for the day. Score the new card's seams there as for a sew, then pay 1 button. A card that covers another can be covered in turn.

Then refill the Rail. Scoring is the same either way:

Seam: 2 buttonsEach card you touch whose touching edge shows the same fabric scores 2 buttons.
Trend: +1A matching seam in the Trend fabric scores 1 more button.
Perfect fit: +2If your card touches 2 or more cards and every touching edge matches, take 2 more buttons.

Edges that don't match score nothing and cost nothing. The Foundation scores nothing, but it can be covered. Every card is sewn or upcycled: there is no pass and no discard. Taking a card is also how you keep it from your rival.

Effects. After you sew or upcycle a card, score it, then resolve its effect. Effects are not optional, except that you may choose not to turn a card with Seam ripper or not to swap with Remnant sale. Second pair of hands and Rummage only fire for a card you played yourself, not for a card they handed you. A card you are given must be sewn or upcycled, scoring as usual.

Examples

Sew. The Trend is Silk. Your card's left edge (Wool) meets a Wool edge: 2 buttons. Its top edge (Silk) meets a Silk edge: 2, plus 1 for the Trend. Both match, so it is a perfect fit: +2. That turn scores 7.

Upcycle. You sew the Kimono (Tops) over a Pocket Tee (Tops) whose seams were poor. Its edges match two of the Tee's old neighbours: 2 + 2, perfect fit +2, minus 1 for the remake: 5 buttons, then the Tops effect fires.

Two days, and nothing is wasted

A day ends when the deck and the Rail are empty. Washing up: unpick the whole quilt, covered cards included, and shuffle all 18 cards together. Your buttons stay banked. Day 2 is played with the same clothes, remade: a new Foundation, a new Trend, a new Rail. Player 2 starts day 2.

Score. Buttons from both days. Most buttons wins. A tie is a shared win.

Solo

Use a Rail of 3. After each of your turns the oldest Rail card goes cold and leaves the day, so you play 9 cards a day. Play both days and score as above. A typical game scores around 87.
